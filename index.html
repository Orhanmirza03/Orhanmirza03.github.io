Personal research project

AI-Augmented Navigation Tools

Background and context

The rapid deployment of offshore wind farms across the North Sea, Baltic Sea, and beyond has created structurally constrained maritime corridors in which collision risk is already elevated relative to open water.

Electronic Chart Display and Information Systems (ECDIS) remain the primary navigational decision-support tool under IMO SOLAS Regulation V/19. However, standard ECDIS implementations face critical limitations in dynamic offshore wind energy area environments, including radar tracking degradation, limited integration of probabilistic risk models, opaque alert generation, and an inability to learn from historical traffic patterns.

This project explores how artificial intelligence and explainable AI can augment ECDIS for safer vessel transit through offshore wind energy areas.

Group project

Customer-Centric App for Port Services

A service-request workflow for port customers, sales representatives, vessel crews, internal departments, and accounting.

  1. A customer initiates a service request by email, including the requested service and time interval.
  2. A sales representative checks availability and responds with an application form.
  3. The customer completes the form and submits it with advance-payment confirmation.
  4. The sales representative notifies the relevant internal department to reserve the necessary vessel.
  5. The customer coordinates with the vessel crew when changes are required.
  6. The crew records time spent and fuel used.
  7. The sales representative consolidates the data in the internal document-management system.
  8. Accounting generates an invoice from the compiled documentation.
